## Security Policy

Security matters to us, and we appreciate anyone who takes the time to report a problem responsibly.

Supported Versions

**0xColony is a young organization and its projects are still evolving.**

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, pull requests, or Discord channels.

Use one of these private channels instead:

0xColony's discord vulnerability forum or/
Email: send details to dpnmbhatt@proton.me.

Please include as much of the following as you can:

The repository and version/commit affected
A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact
Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
Any suggested fix, if you have one
What to Expect

Colony is volunteer-run, so we can't promise enterprise response times, but we will do our best to:

Acknowledge your report within 7 days
Share an assessment (accepted, needs more info, or declined) within 14 days
Keep you updated while a fix is being worked on
Credit you in the fix or release notes, if you'd like

## We do not currently offer a bug bounty.

## Disclosure

We ask that you give us a reasonable amount of time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly. We're happy to coordinate a disclosure date with you once a fix is ready.

## Scope

This policy covers code and projects hosted under the 0xColony GitHub organization. Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies should be reported to their respective maintainers, though we appreciate a heads-up if they affect our projects.

## Good Faith

We will not pursue action against anyone who researches and reports vulnerabilities in good faith: avoiding privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption, and giving us a chance to respond before going public.

Thank you for helping keep Colony and its community safe.
